NPass is an offline password manager: all your accounts in an encrypted vault on your phone, protected by a single master password. No account, no server, not even Internet access: your passwords never leave your device.

★ ALL YOUR ACCOUNTS IN ONE PLACE
• For each account: title, username, password, website and notes
• Custom fields for a PIN code, a security question…, hidden if you like
• Categories and favorites, search, sort by name or by last update
• Copy a password in one tap: it is hidden from the keyboard suggestions and cleared from the clipboard after 30 seconds

★ BUILT-IN 2FA CODES
Turn on two-factor authentication on your accounts: NPass shows the codes (TOTP), like an authenticator app. Scan the QR code shown by the website, import a screenshot of it, or paste the key or the otpauth:// link. Your 2FA keys are encrypted with the rest of the account and included in your backups.

★ STRONG PASSWORDS
• Password generator: length, uppercase, lowercase, digits, symbols, without ambiguous characters
• Passphrases: words that are easy to remember and hard to guess
• Strength meter for your master password
• Reuse alert: NPass tells you when the same password protects several accounts

★ SECURITY
• AES-256-GCM encryption, with a key derived from your master password by Argon2id
• Your master password is never stored: nobody, not even Netersoft, can read your accounts. Choose it carefully, it cannot be recovered
• Optional fingerprint or face unlock
• Auto-lock after the delay you choose, or as soon as you leave the app
• Screenshots blocked, and the app hidden in the recent apps screen

★ ENCRYPTED BACKUPS
Export your accounts to a file encrypted with the password of your choice, keep it on your computer or in the cloud, and restore it on a new phone, merging it or replacing the vault.

★ FREE AND RESPECTFUL OF YOUR PRIVACY
• No ads, no account, no data collected
• Works fully offline
• Light and dark theme
• In English and French
• Free software (GNU GPL v3)

Email, bank, social networks, streaming, Wi-Fi: keep all your passwords safe, and remember only one.
